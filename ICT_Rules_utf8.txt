The ICT Silver Bullet is a time-based trading strategy developed by Michael J. Huddleston (Inner Circle Trader). It focuses on high-probability "algorithmic" price delivery that occurs during specific one-hour windows when institutional activity is highest.

1. The Silver Bullet Time Windows (EST):
- London Open: 3:00 AM – 4:00 AM
- New York AM: 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM
- New York PM: 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM

2. Core Setup Rules:
- Identify HTF Bias: Determine the overall market direction using Higher Time Frames (H4 or Daily). You only trade in the direction of this bias.
- Wait for the Window: Do nothing until the clock hits the start of one of the three windows above.
- Liquidity Sweep: Price must "raid" or sweep a short-term liquidity level (a previous high or low).
- Displacement & FVG: Look for a sharp, energetic move in the opposite direction of the sweep that creates a Fair Value Gap (FVG) on a lower timeframe (1m, 3m, or 5m).
- Market Structure Shift (MSS): Ideally, the displacement move also breaks a recent swing high/low, confirming a shift in structure.

3. Entry and Exit Criteria:
- Entry: Place a limit order at the beginning (or 50% "consequent encroachment") of the Fair Value Gap. The entry must occur within the one-hour window.
- Stop Loss: Place your stop loss above/below the candle that swept the liquidity or the high/low of the FVG formation.
- Take Profit: Target the next logical pool of liquidity (the "Draw on Liquidity"). Aim for a minimum of 1:2 Risk/Reward.
